Features

So many tools!
Sidekick is stuffed full handy tools for making your builds. Search for set bonuses, compare different enhancement configurations of the same power, plan out an attack chain, see a shopping list for the enhancements in your build, open Mids files, and lots more! Just open the Tools menu and explore what's there.
Save, share, and browse builds!
Sidekick features a database where you can save your builds (both privately and publicly) to a searchable library. You can also save and download your builds as files, create share links, and create a post-friendly PNG image that summarizes your build.
Incarnate salvage calculator!
Choose your incarnate powers and get an interative checklist of every component you need to craft them.

Sidekick is a free City of Heroes character build planner that supports Homecoming, Rebirth and Thunderspy. It is built in Rust and Dioxus to be cross platform and accessible for everyone.
