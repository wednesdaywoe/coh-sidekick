Open decisions for the gate (henchman-stats-3):
  • Damage-meter basis — per-attack numbers only (near-free) · best-attack spam proxy (shown here, cheap, labeled) · full rotation/uptime model (a modeling commitment: pet AI, lifespan, level offset).
  • What else lands on the main tab — pet survivability headline? roster count? or damage only, with the rest on the pet tab?
  • Pet tab placement — a tab on the Info panel, a section on the summon card, or both (card summary → tab detail)?
  • Level offset — henchmen may sit below the MM's level in game; tables here read at L50. Measure the offset before promising exact numbers.
